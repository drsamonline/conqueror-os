The app

What it does not do

No server, no account, no telemetry. The app makes no network requests after it loads — open it with the network off and every system still works.

Your reading, your journal and your streak stay in the browser on your own device. There is nothing to sign into and nothing to sync, which also means there is nothing to lose to someone else's outage.
