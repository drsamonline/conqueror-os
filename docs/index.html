// What it does

A practice system, not just a reader

Six systems in one file. Everything works offline and stores to your own device — the app makes no network requests after it loads.

Reading system

A multi-book library with parts and chapters, and a reader built for study.

  • Inline highlights in amber / blue / green with notes
  • Bookmarks, favourite quotes, focus mode, read-aloud
  • Inline SVG diagrams and dwell-time tracking

Journal & reflection

Six entry types with tags, filters, dictation and image attachments.

  • Draft autosave and edit-in-place
  • Rotating daily prompt and "on this day" resurfacing
  • Mood per entry, stored in IndexedDB for attachments

Trackers & analytics

Built-in and custom trackers with real local-only analytics.

  • Trend line + 7-day moving average, calendar heatmap
  • Pearson correlation between any two trackers
  • Printable and exportable reports

Challenges & review

Structured programs plus spaced repetition so lessons stick.

  • 7 / 21 / 30 / 45 / 90-day programs and custom challenges
  • Pause, resume, reminders, shareable progress card
  • SM-2-lite review over each chapter's key idea

Worksheets & knowledge

Fillable sheets with saved history, plus a graph of what connects to what.

  • Daily Check, Weekly Review, Setback Ledger
  • Knowledge graph of chapters, worksheets, challenges
  • Glossary auto-built from chapter tags

Data, safety & privacy

Your data stays on your device — and you can always get it out.

  • Undo on every destructive action; PIN app lock
  • Encrypted backup (AES-GCM + PBKDF2), JSON/MD/Word/CSV export
  • Import with merge-or-replace; CSP blocks all outbound calls

// FAQ

Questions, answered

Does it need a server or an account? +
No. Conqueror OS is a single self-contained HTML document with no backend and no accounts. Open it and it runs; your data is stored locally in your browser.
Does it send my data anywhere? +
No. After the page loads there are no network requests at all — enforced with a Content-Security-Policy that sets connect-src 'none'. There is no analytics, telemetry or tracking.
Can I use it offline? +
Yes — that is the point. It works from file://, a USB stick, a static host or an intranet share. An optional service worker for installable use when served over HTTP is on the roadmap.
Where is my data stored? +
In your browser's localStorage on your device only, with IndexedDB used for image attachments. Storage is bounded by the browser's quota (roughly 5 MB); the app shows a usage meter and warns as it fills.
Is it free? +
The software (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) is MIT-licensed — free to use, modify and redistribute. The written content (books, exercises, prompts and interface copy) is an original work, © all rights reserved. See the repo's content licence.
Which browsers are supported? +
Modern evergreen browsers from around 2019 onward: Chrome 73+, Edge 79+, Firefox 62+ and Safari 12+. It uses ES2019 features and needs no transpilation.
Is it a medical or therapy tool? +
No. Conqueror OS is a self-help and reflection tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical or psychological diagnosis or treatment. If you are struggling with your mental health, please speak to a qualified professional.
